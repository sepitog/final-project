Ontario-Specific Tips

  • McMaster only considers your MCAT CARS score (min. 123). Your overall MCAT matters less here.
  • NOSM does not require the MCAT at all. Prioritises Northern Ontario applicants.
  • UofT has a hard GPA cutoff of 3.6 on the OMSAS scale — below this, your file is not reviewed.
  • CASPer is required by McMaster, Queen's, and Ottawa. Prepare regardless of your stats.
  • Ontario schools accept under 10% of applicants — every component of your application must work together.
